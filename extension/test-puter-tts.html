Sample Documentation

This is sample text that would be read by LexiFlow. The extension splits content into sentences and reads them one by one with highlighting.

Each sentence is highlighted as it's spoken, and you can click anywhere to jump to that sentence. The view auto-scrolls to keep the current sentence at eye level.

Voice options include high-quality neural voices from Puter.js, or offline browser voices as a fallback.
